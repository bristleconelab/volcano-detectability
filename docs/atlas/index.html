About

For every Smithsonian GVP Holocene volcano: the smallest Mogi point-source volume change at 2, 5 or 10 km depth that would exceed the daily white noise (1σ) of at least one open Nevada Geodetic Laboratory GNSS station, after USGS SIR 2024-5062-D and 2026-5117-A; and the change whose line-of-sight uplift reaches 5.9 cm (Biggs et al. 2022) at the nearest coherent Sentinel-1 pixel (seasonal 12-day coherence, Kellndorfer et al. 2022).

This is what open data can see. Many observatories run GNSS networks that are not public, so "blind" here means "not visible in open data", not "unmonitored".

Retrospective sensitivity only; no eruption forecasts. Code and tables: github.com/ismoilovdevml/volcano-detectability. Data: GVP (Smithsonian, non-commercial terms), NGL (Blewitt et al. 2018), Kellndorfer et al. 2022, Natural Earth.
